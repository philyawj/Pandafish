# Direct-leaf target alignment: signal gate fails

All 4,474 saved witness histories reconstruct exactly. The fixed training-only
diagnosis selects eight roots per turn from 16 distinct components and retains
all their labeled alternatives. Pin the teacher/settings and retain 85 new
50,000-node queries plus raw UCI records. There are 84 fully finite comparisons.

Median absolute root-versus-witness difference is **45.5cp**, below the frozen
75cp signal target. Eleven of 138 clear preference pairs reverse (7.971%), below
10%. No losing-mate classification changes. The diagnostic passes its coverage
requirement but fails the predeclared signal gate.

The shared runner stops automatically. Remaining label collection, another fit,
runtime activation, native controls and games do not run. The differences are
real, but this small diagnostic does not establish the large mismatch proposed
as the next cause to address. It also does not prove all root/leaf targets are
interchangeable. Finite-node horizon effects and the limited 16-root sample
remain limitations. No threshold, sample or fitter is revised to make it pass.

Source snapshots, selection, queries, alignment metrics and the failed workflow
remain here. [Infrastructure notes](INFRASTRUCTURE.md) retain the pre-query
Homebrew-symlink failure and the pinned-target repair. The engine still plays
released v0.22. Reassess a different architectural opportunity before another
