# Ranking after the revised-kernel result

The current recommendation is to review the exact kernel before starting another
feature. Its measured 35.35% cold / 37.84% total warm savings and 64% / 71% / 70%
screen support practical promotion, with the public short-budget surrogate
failure disclosed. The result does not exhaust fundamental opportunities or
make a book/online dataset a prerequisite. No following item starts automatically.
Reassess after each result; this ranking is not a fixed checklist.

1. **A new low-cost checking-move interface and bounded tactical-horizon proof.**
   Earlier legally verified quiet mates establish a real capture-only horizon
   omission. The old standalone proof failed actual-budget balance and stays
   inactive. A dedicated checking-only generator can potentially avoid building
   and tagging every quiet move, while the new evasion prefilter can reduce the
   legal-escape work. First measure actual candidate/check/proof frequency and
   cost on the resulting engine. Use legal positive proofs, escapes/captures/
   blocks, discovered checks, EP, promotions and both-turn negative controls.
   Require a substantially cheaper new trigger/interface and fresh actual-budget
   evidence. Do not reactivate the old proof, recursively add all quiet checks,
   or claim this will fix HhPI3vCx; that root's cause is not established by the
   completed-depth diagnosis.
2. **A different contextual evaluation representation with a real inference
   budget and untouched final families.** Repeated inspected linear models and
   isolated old safety bonuses do not justify another coefficient adjustment.
   A larger Go-only contextual/nonlinear experiment needs representative offline
   collection, decision-level objectives, strict fitting/development/holdout
   separation and actual-budget selection for both turns. This is a substantive
   research project, not a claim that adding one familiar chess term will win.
   New data may be needed; an opening book or Lichess dataset is not specifically
   required by the present evidence.
3. **Reprofile the new kernel before another exact-state or selectivity project.**
   The old allocation, clock, tag, aspiration/history and reuse rankings cannot
   simply be reused after generation and exchange costs change. Child storage
   removed bytes without a large elapsed gain; the clock's small-scope screen
   failed; within-stage laziness and context deferral had limited opportunity.
   Make/unmake, shared legal-state work or reductions need a new measured
   opportunity and explicit correctness boundary. Conservative depth/history/
   mate safeguards should not be weakened merely to raise a hit rate.

A broader-opening confirmation is useful before a large strength claim or target
advancement, but it is not an automatic new mandatory promotion gate or a rerun
of the same ten-opening screen for a preferred score. Keep every result. The
next release decision and any further experiment remain separate review steps.
