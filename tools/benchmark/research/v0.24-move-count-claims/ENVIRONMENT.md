`GOCACHE=/tmp/pandafish-v016-go-cache`. Before warm timing, power reports AC,
100% charged. No deliberate builds, tests, profiles or other research workloads
overlap warm/native/game timing. External process utilization and temperature

a universal cost bound; the0.117% observation should not be interpreted as a
