[candidate evidence](../benchmark/research/v0.21-rank-order/RESULTS.md) preserves
its original `v0.21-rank-order-dev` label, native v0.20 identity, binary hash,

These are integration checks, not new strength estimates or replacements for
the 50-game-per-opponent candidate screen. The candidate identities/hashes are
not relabelled as the release binary. Stockfish stays Skill 3; no larger
